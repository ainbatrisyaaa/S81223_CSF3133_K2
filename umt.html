About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science before becoming an independent university in 2007.

The university focuses heavily on marine science, aquatic resources, and environmental conservation. Its campus is situated right next to the South China Sea, providing students and researchers direct access to coastal and marine ecosystems.

Today, UMT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various faculties, aiming to produce skilled graduates in science, technology, and management.

Key Achievements

UMT is recognized nationally and internationally for its research in oceanography and marine biodiversity. The university operates its own research vessels and marine research stations to support hands-on learning and scientific discovery.
